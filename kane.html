Independent digital project

Kane9 Club

A fan-focused website built around Harry Kane, combining player stories with collectible digital experiences.

Project purpose

I wanted to turn an interest in football into an experience that people could explore and revisit. The project began with a capsule-style collecting interaction and developed into a broader fan website.

My contribution

I developed the concept, selected the visual direction, planned content and interactions, and refined the experience through repeated revisions. My priorities included clear instructions, readable layouts, and an interface that works on mobile devices.

Experience design

The project explores collectible moments, a personal collection, multilingual presentation, and football stories. A consistent visual theme connects the content and gives visitors a recognizable experience.

What I learned

The project helped me practice turning audience interests into a digital concept, organizing content around a clear theme, and evaluating how individual features contribute to the overall experience.
